# Product context — Alessandro Luiz Sica De Almeida Portfolio

## Product register

This is a personal brand portfolio and evidence-led career site. Its primary job
is to help Alessandro secure an excellent senior role in AI, product,
transformation, analytics, customer experience, marketing technology, games, or
innovation. It is not a generic résumé website, a startup landing page, or a
catalog of unfinished experiments.

The same evidence library may later support a separately prepared immigration
case, including an EB-2 petition strategy, but the public website must not make
legal conclusions or claim eligibility. It should preserve traceability,
dates, authorship, public evidence, and project impact so qualified counsel can
decide what is useful later.

## Primary users and desired outcomes

### Primary audience

- Hiring managers and executive recruiters evaluating Director, Senior
  Director, VP, Head of AI/Product, AI transformation, product strategy, and
  innovation candidates.
- Product, engineering, data, and business leaders who need a leader capable of
  reframing ambiguous problems, inventing new operating models, mobilizing
  multidisciplinary teams, and turning the result into measurable reality.

### Secondary audience

- Potential collaborators, clients, investors, academic partners, and legal
  professionals reviewing a documented body of work.

### Primary conversion

Within three minutes, a visitor should understand Alessandro's positioning,
inspect at least one detailed case study, and choose to contact him, view his
résumé, or open a verifiable public artifact.

## Positioning

Alessandro is an inventive transformation leader. Across his career, he has
identified patterns others missed, reframed difficult operating problems,
created original systems and products, and led the people, technology, and
decisions required to make those solutions real. AI, product, analytics, and
hands-on technical depth are instruments of that leadership, not the headline.

Recommended public descriptor:

> Transformation and AI executive who combines creativity, business vision,
> and AI to solve complex problems with agility, control, and accountable
> implementation.

The narrative should show continuity rather than reinvention: early predictive
analytics, pricing, collections, workflow automation, and enterprise platforms
demonstrate the same creative problem-solving pattern now applied to ML
products, agentic orchestration, and multimodal AI.

## Brand personality

Three voice words: **decisive, inventive, accountable**.

The visual and verbal personality should feel like a well-run product lab with
executive judgment: ambitious but not inflated, technical but not costumed,
confident but explicit about limitations.

### Anti-references

The portfolio must not resemble:

- a generic résumé template with interchangeable skill cards;
- a cyberpunk or neon “AI expert” page;
- a startup promising capabilities without evidence;
- a dense academic CV with no product narrative;
- an agency gallery that hides contribution, maturity, or limitations;
- an immigration marketing page.

The home page must connect these cases through a visual career timeline rather
than treating them as an isolated project catalog. Each career chapter shows

scale indicators, related systems, and current evidence state. The trajectory
must also expose the parallel threads that make the executive profile complete:
teaching and publications, consulting and venture creation, international
operations and transactions, and continuous education and credentials. These
threads must remain visually connected to time rather than becoming a résumé
table or a disconnected credentials grid.

## Language

English is the default language because the immediate hiring market is the
United States. Every visitor-facing page must offer a complete Portuguese
version with equivalent facts and structure. Content must not silently diverge
between languages.

## Accessibility and interaction baseline

- Target WCAG 2.2 AA for contrast, keyboard navigation, focus, headings, forms,
  and meaningful alternative text.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`; no essential information may depend on
  animation.
- Maintain readable body text and clear solution stage/status labels. Internal
  readiness grades must never appear in the public experience.
- Support desktop, laptop, tablet, and mobile without horizontal scrolling.
- Use honest link labels and indicate when an artifact is private, archived,
  public, or pending documentation.

## Evidence and disclosure policy

Every claim belongs to one of four states:

- **Verified public** — may be published with a link or reproducible artifact.
- **Verified local / sanitized** — may be summarized; screenshots and excerpts
  require a privacy and ownership review before publication.
- **Candidate-declared** — may appear only with careful attribution until
  supporting evidence is attached.
- **Private / restricted** — stays in the internal evidence index and is never
  copied to the public repository.

The site must separate product maturity from ambition: prototype, functional
local system, deployed application, public dataset, research in preparation,
and commercial product are not interchangeable.

Never publish credentials, API keys, `.env` files, personal data, customer or
debtor data, private financial information, internal URLs, proprietary datasets,
contract-owned source code, student data, unpublished research results without
approval, or artifacts whose ownership is uncertain.

## Success criteria

- A recruiter can identify three high-value capabilities in under 30 seconds.
- Each featured case states problem, role, decisions, architecture, evidence,
  result or current maturity, and limitations.
- Flagship claims link to public or sanitized evidence where available.
- The repository contains a repeatable intake process for adding older projects
  and a publication-status matrix.
- The site builds locally without secrets and can deploy to GitHub Pages.
- No public artifact overstates authorship, production status, accuracy,
  publication, revenue, adoption, or legal eligibility.
